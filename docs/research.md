US$23,940 is the ten-seat list-price scenario at US$2,394 per year for Minitab Solution Center Core. This is an arithmetic scenario, not a customer invoice, regional quote or guaranteed saving. [Minitab pricing](https://www.minitab.com/en-us/try-buy/) lists single named user annual subscriptions, taxes additional and local pricing in the cart. Analytics is US$2,593.50 and Copilot US$2,793 per user annually. The public page quotes individual prices, not the ten-seat sum.

Scores out of five: annual bill 3, data-shaped 4, our buyers pay for it 2, search demand 4, one-session buildable 4, import path 5. Total 22/30. The customer and local CRM-reference search found no verified Minitab buyer. Buyer fit is a hypothesis for manufacturing and process-improvement teams. Search results show alternative and pricing intent and complaints about named-user costs; no query-volume measure was available.

Compared candidates: IBM SPSS Statistics 21/30 (2,4,2,4,4,5), dmarcian 18/30 (1,4,2,3,4,4), Toolio 19/30 (1,5,2,3,4,4). dmarcian's public Enterprise tier is below five figures annually. Toolio had no verified annual bill in the checked pages. Minitab won on the industry fit and stronger published annual licence value. These are research judgments, not vendor quality rankings.

The family is statistical-process-analysis. Existing manufacturing-quality targets manage quality systems and corrective actions; this build calculates descriptive results, individuals limits and overall capability from ordered numerical measurements. It does not duplicate tonight's lead-routing or vulnerability-remediation families.
